# <PROGRAM> operating instructions

Write documentation, artifacts, and user-facing updates in English.
Read HANDOFF.md, SCOPE.md, RULES.md, and STATE.json before research.

1. Verify authorization, exclusions, identification, and request limits.
2. Choose one useful permitted step and read its previous evidence.
3. Assess side effects before every request, including GET requests.
4. Use only authorized operator-owned accounts; stop at an unmet prerequisite.
5. Record observations, controls, tested conditions, and limitations.
6. Update the target, candidate, state, and short decision journal as needed.
7. Prepare reports for operator review. Delivery requires explicit authorization.

TARGETS.md uses State | Target | Reason, with one full hostname per Target cell.
Candidate headings are ### C-<id> — <observation>, with Asset, Severity,
Disposition, and Status fields. Preserve IDs. Severity is an estimate or Unknown;
record Proposed severity separately. Dispositions are explicit review decisions.
A negative applies to the tested conditions, not every host or route in a class.
